About

Why Whov exists

A private room for serious people. Built to cut through noise, build real relationships and move real capital.

Our Mission

Whov exists to bring together founders, traders, investors and geopolitical thinkers into one high-signal room. We believe the quality of your peer group determines the quality of your decisions — and most online communities optimise for growth, not depth.

What We Are

  • A private, invite-only community for people who build, allocate and analyse.
  • A place for honest conversation about markets, capital, entrepreneurship and geopolitics.
  • A network that spans 40+ countries with regional rooms, ambassadors and rotating events.
  • A room where every contribution is expected to add something.

What We Are Not

  • Not a job board or freelance marketplace.
  • Not a signal group or tip-sharing service.
  • Not a place for financial advice or guaranteed-return claims.
  • Not a mass community. We keep it small on purpose.

Our Principles

Signal over noise

Every channel, every rule and every decision is designed to protect the quality of conversation. Low-effort posts are removed. High-effort contributions are rewarded.

Respect and rigour

Disagreement is welcome. Disrespect is not. We expect members to argue ideas hard and treat people well — the two are not in conflict.

No financial advice

Nothing shared in this circle is financial, legal or tax advice. Every member is responsible for their own decisions. This is non-negotiable.

Global by default

We design for international members from day one — rotating event times, regional channels and language support.

Join

If this sounds like your kind of room, join on Discord. We keep the circle small and the entry deliberate.

Community

Community Rules

Short, clear rules. Break them and access is revoked.

Enforced by moderators, 24/7
The core idea: High signal. Zero noise. Respect everyone. Argue ideas, not people.

1. Respect First

  • No harassment, hate speech, slurs or personal attacks.
  • No discrimination based on race, religion, gender, nationality or identity.
  • Disagree with ideas — never with the person.

2. No Spam, No Promotions

  • No unsolicited DMs promoting products, services or channels.
  • No affiliate links, referral links or "DM me to learn more" posts.
  • Self-promotion only in designated channels and only when relevant.

3. No Financial Advice

  • No "buy this now" calls, guaranteed-return claims or signal services.
  • No pump-and-dump, coordinated buying or market manipulation.
  • All discussion is educational. Members make their own decisions.

4. Keep Politics Civil

  • Political discussion belongs in designated channels only.
  • No inflammatory, extremist or violent content.
  • No fake news or unverified claims presented as fact.

5. Privacy and Consent

  • No doxxing — do not share anyone's personal information without consent.
  • No screenshots of private conversations shared publicly without consent.
  • Respect confidentiality of private rooms and mastermind pods.

6. No Illegal Activity

  • No content promoting illegal activity.
  • No sharing of pirated content, stolen data or stolen financial information.
  • No attempts to defraud or scam other members.

7. Stay On Topic

  • Post in the correct channel. Off-topic posts are moved or removed.
  • Search before posting. Duplicate threads may be merged or removed.
  • Keep voice rooms focused — no talking over others.

8. Enforcement

Violations are handled progressively:

  • First warning — for minor or accidental violations.
  • Mute — for repeated or moderate violations.
  • Kick / Ban — for serious violations including scams, harassment, doxxing or financial advice.

Moderators have final say. Appeals can be made via Contact.

9. Reporting

If you see a violation, report it to a moderator rather than engaging directly. Do not reply to spam or harassment.

1. Not Financial Advice

All content shared in Whov — including messages, voice discussions, shared documents, charts and analyses — is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

We are not registered investment advisors, brokers, dealers or portfolio managers in any jurisdiction. Nothing in the Community should be interpreted as a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security, asset or financial instrument.

2. Trading and Investment Risk

Trading and investing carry substantial risk. You may lose some or all of your capital. Past performance — whether of a member, a strategy or an asset — is never indicative of future results.

Leverage, derivatives and crypto assets carry amplified risk. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

3. Crypto Risk

Digital assets are highly volatile and largely unregulated in many jurisdictions. Risks include:

  • Extreme price volatility and total loss of capital.
  • Smart contract bugs, exploits and protocol failures.
  • Loss of access due to lost keys or compromised wallets.
  • Regulatory changes, tax implications and unclear legal status.
  • Scams, rug pulls, phishing and fake projects.
  • Stablecoin de-peg events — even "stable" assets can lose their peg under stress.

4. No Guarantees

No member, moderator, founder or contributor of Whov guarantees any outcome, result or return. Anyone claiming guaranteed returns is violating our Community Rules and should be reported immediately.

5. Do Your Own Research

Before making any financial decision:

  • Conduct your own independent research.

  • Understand the risks specific to your situation.
